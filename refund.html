Refund policy

NOVIQ resources are digital files, so they cannot be handed back once delivered. If something goes wrong with your order, this page explains how we put it right.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

When you get a full refund

  • Faulty files. A file will not open or is corrupted, and we could not send you a working replacement within 3 business days.
  • Not as described. Something listed under “What’s included” on the product page is missing from what we delivered.
  • Paid twice or the wrong amount. We refund the duplicate payment or the difference.
  • Not delivered. Your payment was confirmed but we were unable to deliver the files.
  • Cancelled before delivery. If you ask us to cancel before your files are sent, we refund in full.

What is not covered

  • A change of mind after the files have been delivered, because the files cannot be returned.
  • Free resources, which cost nothing.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after you received your files.
  • Files that have been shared, resold or redistributed to others.

How to ask for a refund

Use the contact form, choose the topic Support, and include the name and email you ordered with, your order reference and a short description of the problem. Please write within 7 days of receiving your files. We reply within 2 business days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the same account or UPI ID the payment came from, usually within 5 to 7 business days. Banks sometimes take a little longer to show the credit.

Bundles

A bundle is a single order. If one file in a bundle has a problem, we fix or replace that file first. If we cannot, the bundle is refunded as a whole.

Before you buy: every product page lists the contents, level, formats and prerequisites, and the free library lets you judge the quality first.
