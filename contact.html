How do I order?
  1. Explore. Browse Explore, Bundles or the free library.
  2. Select a pack. Open a resource, check what's inside, then choose Buy now (or Add to cart).
  3. Follow the steps. Checkout shows what you're buying and the total, in three short steps: Pay, Confirm, Get files.
  4. Pay by UPI. Scan the QR code (or tap Open UPI app on your phone), then enter your email and the 12-digit transaction ID. We email your files once the payment is confirmed.
Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI. Pay with any UPI app, such as GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM.

I paid, but I haven't received my files

Every order is matched to its payment by hand, so files are sent once we've confirmed it. Meanwhile, check your spam folder and that the email you entered is right. Still nothing? Choose Delivery issue in the form and include your order reference and transaction ID.

My payment went through but I lost the order reference

No problem. Choose Payment help, then send the UPI transaction ID, the amount and the resource name, and we'll match it to your order.

I entered the wrong email or transaction ID

Write to us with your order reference and the correct details (topic Order enquiry) and we'll fix it.

How is my file delivered?

Privately by email, after your payment is confirmed. Previews never contain the complete paid file.

Which formats do you offer?

PDF guides and workbooks, Notion templates, and HTML/CSS/JS starter files. Every product page lists its formats.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. The free library has resources you can download without paying.

What about refunds?

Files are digital, so they can't be returned once delivered, but we refund faulty or missing files, duplicate payments and orders that could not be delivered. Write to us within 7 days of receiving your files. Approved refunds go back to the same account or UPI ID, usually within 5 to 7 business days. Read the full refund policy.
